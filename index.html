A parent group.

Altaş Holdings Limited brings airline brands together within the Roblox aviation community.

WHO WE ARE

Built to support the brands
within our group.

Altaş focuses on airline ownership, leadership and development. Our role is to give the group a clear direction and provide shared support where it helps our companies.

Our portfolio currently includes Corendon Airlines Roblox. Each airline maintains its own identity, experience and community, with Altaş providing support at group level.
