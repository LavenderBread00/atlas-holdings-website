04 / Our Approach

Clear relationships. Stronger brands.

We make an important distinction between the airlines Atlas owns and the independent organisations we work alongside.

Group subsidiaries

Airlines owned by Atlas Holdings Limited and operating as part of our group.

  • Part of the Atlas portfolio
  • Shared group strategy and support
  • Individual airline brand identity

Independent partners

Separate organisations that may collaborate with Atlas without becoming subsidiaries.

  • Maintain independent ownership
  • Explore relevant collaboration opportunities
  • Retain their own leadership and operations
